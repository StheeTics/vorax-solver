# Optimal Play

How to build a run in Surgical Preparation that reaches 840,000 (the end-of-run battle bonus turns that into the rainbow reward). A run is shaped by its first instrument: pick it, then play the loop below with that instrument's partners and potions.

**Contents:** [The play loop](#the-play-loop) · [Choosing your first instrument](#choosing-your-first-instrument) · [Incubation Sac](#incubation-sac) · [Chrysalis Specimen](#chrysalis-specimen) · [Contracted Claws](#contracted-claws) · [The other first instruments](#the-other-first-instruments) · [Instruments to skip](#instruments-to-skip) · [How these numbers were made](#how-these-numbers-were-made)

## The play loop

**Score is Activity × Quantity, added up over all six tanks.** Every move is about growing both, on the groups that matter.

1. **Round 1: your first instrument.** Three basic instruments are offered; pick one (see below), or refresh if none of the good ones is there.
2. **Rounds 1–10: one potion a round.** Each round, three potions are offered and you play one. Play the one that feeds your instrument and grows your biggest groups. Refresh the hand only when nothing in it helps (about 1 hand in 6), and open a Potion Kit instead of refreshing: it's a fresh choice that keeps your refresh.
3. **Two more instruments, at 8,000 and 28,000.** Reaching each score brings an advanced instrument offer, usually in rounds 2–5: most runs have all three instruments by round 5, so get there early.
4. **Rounds 11–13: the instruments work alone.** No more potions; what you built keeps growing.
5. **The final battle** turns your board into the reward: 840,000 or more is the rainbow.

Throughout: **add and fuse.** The strongest runs keep adding monsters and turn them into a few very large groups, then pile Quantity and Activity onto those.

## Incubation Sac

**+45 Quantity to every monster each time a monster is added, once you have 2 or more Pest groups.** It pays off every time you add a monster, all game long. 82% of runs reach 840,000.

**Leans on:** Pests (keep at least 2 Pest groups) and anything that adds monsters.

**Next instruments**

| Partner | Runs | Reached 840k |
|---|---|---|
| **Beak of the Twice-Born** | 124 | **95%** |
| **Black Goat Gut Suture** | 335 | **88%** |
| Pig Iron Bone Saw | 201 | 83% |
| Tanned Leather Restraint | 154 | 82% |
| Canine Teeth File | 113 | 81% |
| Rawhide Restraint | 96 | 80% |
| Fouled Scraper | 189 | 77% |
| Lady of the Plague Quarter | 65 | 72% |
| Nettle Toggle, Sloughed Cortex | 76 | 63–66% |

- **Beak of the Twice-Born** adds a monster every round, so it triggers the Sac every round.
- **Black Goat Gut Suture** fuses three monsters of the same rarity into one of the next rarity: the Sac keeps making monsters, and the Suture turns them into big ones.

**Best builds:** Black Goat Gut Suture + Canine Teeth File (97% of 35 runs), Beak of the Twice-Born + Fouled Scraper (96%), Beak + Black Goat (93%), Pig Iron Bone Saw + Tanned Leather Restraint (91%), Black Goat + Tanned Leather (89%), Black Goat + Pig Iron (87% of 82 runs, the most common).

**Potions:** Insect Attractant (played 76% of the times it's offered), Xenopanacea: Osteotrophy (67%), Slough Solution (63%), Xeno-Hormone (58%), Potion Kits (52–54%). Rarely: Anesthetic Tincture - Aemberon, Plague Quarter Peat Poultice, Osteolytic Ointment, Osteogenic Powder (5–6%).

**How to play it**
- **Get to 2 Pest groups as early as possible.** Until then the Sac does nothing.
- **Keep a tank free.** An add with all six tanks full fails, and a failed add doesn't trigger the Sac.
- **Clear and refill:** Xeno-Hormone and Slough Solution empty tanks for more adds.

## Chrysalis Specimen

**At the end of each round, +8 Quantity to random groups, repeated as many times as you have Pest groups** (with 5 Pest groups, 25 boosts a round, every round). 82% of runs reach 840,000.

**Leans on:** keeping many Pest groups, and growing the big ones.

**Next instruments**

| Partner | Runs | Reached 840k |
|---|---|---|
| **Pig Iron Bone Saw** | 131 | **90%** |
| **Canine Teeth File** | 83 | **90%** |
| **Fouled Scraper** | 163 | 86% |
| Tanned Leather Restraint | 138 | 84% |
| Lady of the Plague Quarter | 31 | 81% |
| Rawhide Restraint | 72 | 78% |
| Clustered Eggs | 63 | 75% |
| Beak of the Twice-Born | 43 | 74% |
| Nettle Toggle | 49 | 69% |
| Black Goat Gut Suture | 35 | **63%** |

- **Pig Iron Bone Saw, Canine Teeth File and Fouled Scraper** reward monsters that are already big, which is what the Chrysalis's boosts make.
- **Black Goat Gut Suture is a poor partner here,** likely because fusing groups away cuts the number of Pest groups the Chrysalis counts.

**Best builds:** Fouled Scraper + Tanned Leather Restraint (100% of 24 runs), Fouled Scraper + Pig Iron Bone Saw (94%), Canine Teeth File + Pig Iron Bone Saw (91%), Pig Iron Bone Saw + Rawhide Restraint (88%), Pig Iron Bone Saw + Tanned Leather Restraint (86%). Avoid Fouled Scraper + Nettle Toggle (65%).

**Potions:** Twin Hormone - Pest (65%), Bewitching Tincture (62%), Xenopanacea: Osteotrophy (62%), Viscous Bile Solution (59%), Potion Kits (55%). Rarely: Anesthetic Tincture - Aemberon (1%), Gray Matter Spinal Solution, Compound Revitalizing Pill, Fresh Spinal Powder (4%): they turn your Pests into something else.

**How to play it**
- **Build Pest groups early,** even when another card gives more right now: the Chrysalis grows with every Pest group, every round, for the rest of the run.
- **Don't fuse or mutate your Pests away.**

## Contracted Claws

**+150 Quantity to a random monster each time a non-Bone Sentry monster is removed.** It doesn't need Bone Sentries: it's a removal engine. 75% of runs reach 840,000.

**Leans on:** removing monsters (anything but Bone Sentries), and making more to remove.

**Next instruments**

| Beak of the Twice-Born | 82 | 79% |

| Pig Iron Bone Saw | 177 | 78% |
| Sinew Garrote | 108 | 76% |
| Rawhide Restraint | 79 | 76% |
| Vermicular Spinal Cord | 41 | 76% |

**Best builds:** Canine Teeth File + Sinew Garrote (100% of 18 runs), Canine Teeth File + Pig Iron Bone Saw (90%), Fouled Scraper + Rawhide Restraint (88%), Black Goat Gut Suture + Canine Teeth File (87%), Black Goat + Pig Iron (84%). Avoid Fouled Scraper + Sloughed Cortex (38%).

**Potions:** Xenopanacea: Osteotrophy (64%), Insect Attractant (64%), Slough Solution (61%), Xeno-Hormone (59%: removes 1–2, then adds 4 more to remove later), Potion Kit (Large) (57%), Xenopanacea: Brood (56%). Rarely: Anesthetic Tincture - Aemberon (5%), Probiotic Enzyme Solution (6%), Eggshell Powder (8%), Osteogenic Powder (9%).

**How to play it**
- **Feed and remove:** add cheap monsters, then remove them. Every non-Bone Sentry removed is +150 Quantity.
- **Pile the Quantity onto your highest-Activity group:** that's where it's worth the most.

## The other first instruments

Rarely the best on offer, but sometimes the only good one:

- **Adherent Metatarsals** (+180 Quantity to a random monster each time a monster is removed, once you have 2 Bone Sentries; 80% of 93 runs). Partners: Sinew Garrote (90%), Pig Iron Bone Saw (83%), Fouled Scraper (82%). Potions: Slough Solution, Xeno-Hormone, Potion Kits, Bewitching Tincture.
- **Hyperplastic Frontal Lobe** (+80 Activity to a Rare monster each round, once you have 2 Agitos; 76% of 76 runs). Partners: Rawhide Restraint (88%), Canine Teeth File (76%). Potions: Bewitching Tincture, Mutagenic Powder, Vivigenic Powder, Xenopurge Powder.
- **Hypertrophic Pituitary** (+80 Activity to a random monster each round, once you have a Rare or Boss Agito; 84% of 25 runs). Get a Rare Agito first: Bewitching Tincture, Mutagenic Powder, Anesthetic Tincture - Agito.
- **Aberrant Sprout** (+35 Activity to every monster each time one mutates into an Aemberon; 64% of 44 runs). Potions: Fresh Spinal Powder, Viscous Bile Solution, Anesthetic Tincture - Aemberon, Green Bile Solution, Petrified Spinal Solution.
- **Prismatic Liver** is almost never worth taking: refresh instead.

## Instruments to skip

Whatever your first instrument, **Nettle Toggle, Sloughed Cortex and Two-Inch Skull Nail** rarely help (with the first two, 60–64% of runs reach 840,000, against 75–90% for the good partners; the Solver almost never takes the third). Take them only when nothing else on offer fits.

**Xenopanacea** cards change a random monster into an Aemberon and give it +333 Activity (Osteotrophy) or +333 Quantity (Brood). On your biggest monster that's huge; on a small one it's minor, and it can break a build that needs Pests or a rarity triple. Take one most of the time, not always.

## How these numbers were made

- **2,000 simulated runs,** played by the Solver's own advice: every option played out to the end of the run 500 times, at the live game's measured odds (October 25, 2026). The engine is this project's simulator; its rules and odds are checked against boards from real games.
- **Runs, not causes.** A partner's numbers include the boards it was chosen on: the Solver takes an instrument when the board suits it. Read them as "how runs went when the advice chose this", and builds with few runs (under about 30) as rough.
- **Real games agree on the leaders:** in 3,841 games played with the Solver (October 19–23), Incubation Sac and Contracted Claws were the most common first picks and Black Goat Gut Suture the most common and best-scoring advanced instrument.
- **The rules can differ from the game.** If a card works differently in the game than in the simulator, the numbers for it are off; please [report it](https://github.com/tidewarden/tli-vorax-simulator/issues/new/choose).
